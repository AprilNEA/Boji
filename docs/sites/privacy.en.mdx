AprilNEA LLC ("**we**", "**us**" or "**our**") is the developer of Boji (also known as "薄肌", "薄肌俱乐部" and "Lean Muscle Club"). This Privacy Policy (this "**Policy**") describes how we process your personal information when you use Boji and the rights available to you.

**Please read this Policy carefully before you use Boji. Provisions concerning sensitive personal information, cross-border transfers of personal information and other matters that materially affect your rights are shown in bold.**

This Policy is published in English and Chinese. In the event of any inconsistency, the English version prevails.

## 1. Scope [#scope]

1.1 This Policy applies to the applications, websites and related online services that we provide under the names above (collectively, the "**Services**").

1.2 This Policy does not apply to products or services provided by third parties. Their processing of your personal information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect and How We Use It [#collection-and-use]

2.1 We process your personal information lawfully, fairly, in good faith and only to the extent necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. The table below sets out the main categories of personal information and the purposes for which we process them.

| Category | Personal information | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account information | Account identifiers, contact details and sign-in credentials, including information provided by a third-party account that you use to sign in | Registration, sign-in, authentication and service notices |
| Membership and service information | Membership details, records of your acknowledgment of applicable rules, and the content that you submit or create through the Services | Providing, administering and improving the features that you use |
| **Records that you sync** | **The exercise, sleep and body-related records that you choose to sync** | Syncing them across your devices and displaying them to you |
| Device and service information | Device and service identifiers, such as notification tokens; device information; log information | Delivering notifications, keeping content that you add to your devices up to date, and operating the Services |
| Security and diagnostic information | Log information and diagnostic information | Protecting accounts and the Services, and finding and fixing faults |
| Communications | Contact details and the content of communications | Handling your inquiries, requests and complaints |

2.2 You can use the basic features of the Services without an account. Content that you log in the app is stored locally on your device by default and is not transmitted to us unless you turn on cloud sync or use a feature that requires it. **If you decline to provide personal information that is necessary for a feature, you will not be able to use that feature, but you can continue to use the other features.**

2.3 **Data that you authorize the app to read from a health data platform, such as Apple Health, is not transmitted to our servers without your separate consent and is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining.**

2.4 **Records uploaded through cloud sync constitute sensitive personal information. If disclosed or used unlawfully, they could harm your personal dignity or personal safety. We process them solely for the purpose of syncing and obtain your separate consent before you turn on cloud sync.** You may turn off cloud sync at any time.

2.5 Under applicable law, we may process your personal information without your consent where the processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract to which you are a party, to fulfill a statutory duty or legal obligation, or in other circumstances provided by law.

2.6 If the purpose or method of processing or the types of personal information change, we will inform you in advance and obtain your consent again where required by law.

## 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies [#cookies]

We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers or similar technologies to track you or to deliver advertising. Our websites may store settings in your browser that are necessary for the websites to function.

## 4. Entrusted Processing, Disclosure, Transfer and Publication [#sharing]

4.1 **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not disclose it for targeted advertising.**

4.2 We engage third parties ("**processors**") to provide cloud computing, hosting and error monitoring services. The names, countries or regions, categories of personal information processed and purposes of processing of our processors are set out in the [Third-Party Data Sharing List](./third-parties.mdx). We agree with each processor on the purpose, method and protection obligations of processing, and we supervise their processing activities.

4.3 We do not disclose your personal information to other controllers or make it public, except as described in Section 4.5, with your separate consent, as required by laws or regulations, for litigation or arbitration, or at the request of administrative or judicial authorities.

4.4 If we need to transfer your personal information because of a merger, division, dissolution, bankruptcy or similar event, we will inform you of the name and contact details of the recipient and require the recipient to continue to perform the obligations under this Policy.

4.5 **Certain features of the Services rely on services provided by the operator of your device's platform, such as notification and digital wallet services. When you use such a feature, we exchange with that operator the personal information necessary to provide the feature, such as device and service identifiers. The operator processes that information under its own privacy policy. The operators are set out in the [Third-Party Data Sharing List](./third-parties.mdx).**

## 5. Cross-Border Transfers [#international-transfers]

**The servers of the Services are located in the United States, and our processors may store some personal information in other countries or regions. Wherever you are located, your personal information will be stored and processed in the United States and those other countries or regions, whose data protection laws may differ from the laws where you are located. We take the protective measures required by applicable law.**

## 6. Storage and Security [#storage-and-security]

6.1 We retain your personal information only for the shortest period necessary to achieve the purposes of processing, unless laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise. Upon expiry of that period, we delete or anonymize your personal information.

6.2 We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss. **No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your personal information.**

6.3 If a personal information security incident occurs or may occur, we will take remedial measures immediately and notify you and the competent authorities as required by applicable law.

6.4 If we discontinue the Services, we will stop collecting your personal information, notify you, and delete or anonymize your personal information.

## 7. Your Rights [#your-rights]

7.1 Subject to applicable law, you have the right to access, copy, correct, complete and delete your personal information; to restrict or object to its processing; to withdraw your consent; to close your account; to receive a copy of your personal information or request its transfer; and to ask us to explain our processing rules.

7.2 **You may close your account in the app or by contacting us as set out in Section 10. We will complete the closure within 15 business days after verifying your identity and delete or anonymize your personal information, unless laws or administrative regulations provide otherwise. A closed account cannot be restored.**

7.3 Withdrawal of consent does not affect the validity of processing carried out on the basis of your consent before the withdrawal.

7.4 You may exercise these rights by contacting us as set out in Section 10. We may need to verify your identity and will respond within 15 business days. If we decline your request, we will explain our reasons; you may ask us to reconsider, lodge a complaint with the competent authority or bring legal proceedings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 8. Minors [#minors]

**The Services are intended only for adults aged 18 or older.** We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors and will promptly delete any such information that we discover.
